Important information

GLOBIS HR CONSULTANCY FZE LLC requires the following documents to facilitate approval from the Department of Overseas Employment and the pertinent government ministries of the respective home countries in which we operate.

For employers

Required documents

Essential paperwork for overseas recruitment.

Document 1

Demand letter

A letter addressed to GLOBIS HR CONSULTANCY FZE LLC, specifying:

  • Number of workers required in each category
  • Monthly salaries for each position
  • Contract period
  • Duty hours
  • Air passage arrangements
  • All other benefits for workers
Document 2

Power of attorney

An authorisation letter to GLOBIS HR CONSULTANCY FZE LLC to:

  • Conduct manpower recruitment
  • Handle all formalities in arranging interviews
  • Sign required documents and employment contracts
  • Comply with home country regulations
  • Arrange passports and travel
  • Facilitate travel to the country of employment
Document 3

Offer letter

An official letter carrying all details of the job offer:

  • Job description
  • Salary package
  • Accommodation details
  • Medical insurance coverage
  • Air ticket provisions
  • Vacation entitlements and other allowances
Document 4

Agreement letter

Outlines the terms and conditions of service between the employer and GLOBIS HR CONSULTANCY FZE LLC.

It establishes the formal business relationship and defines the responsibilities, obligations and expectations of both parties throughout the recruitment process.
